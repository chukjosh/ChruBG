    Touch up

    Paint over the image. Erase removes parts you want gone. Restore brings back parts that were removed by mistake, which show faded.

    Crop and resize

    Optional. Drag the image to position it, use Zoom to resize it inside the frame, then choose an output size. Images you do not crop download as usual. Rules for official photos differ by country, and some agencies reject replaced backgrounds, so check theirs before you submit.
